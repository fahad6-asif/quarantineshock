On March 5, 2026, the Animal and Plant Health Inspection Service (APHIS) and the California Department of Food and Agriculture (CDFA) established a Mexican fruit fly ( Anastrepha ludens; Mexfly) quarantine in La Mesa, San Diego County, California, designated the La Mesa quarantine.

APHIS and CDFA established the La Mesa quarantine in response to the February 24 detection of a wild mated female Mexfly from a trap in a loquat tree on a residential property. The quarantine encompassed approximately 77 square miles and contained no commercial agriculture.
