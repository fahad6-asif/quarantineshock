# title: Spring Valley expanded to El Cajon

On June 18, CDFA expanded the Spring Valley quarantine in response to the June 12 detection of one wild mated female from a trap in a grapefruit tree on a residential property in the city of El Cajon. The amended quarantine encompassed approximately 111 square miles and contained 338.5 acres of commercial agriculture that includes avocado, citrus, stone fruit, and tomatoes.

The emergency program area is the entirety of the Mexfly quarantine area within San Diego County, which became effective on June 18, 2026. The emergency program area encompasses all properties within a 4.5-mile radius of all Mexfly detections.

The quarantine restricts movement of host fruits and plants for growers, wholesalers, retailers, and nurseries. Home gardeners are urged to consume homegrown produce onsite and avoid transporting fruit off their property to prevent spreading the pest.
